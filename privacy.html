The RFP Sheet

Privacy

Last updated November 10, 2026

The RFP Sheet is a weekly email published by Cold Zone Studios, 22 Hancock Street, Boston, MA 02114. This page says what we collect, why, and how to stop it.

What we collect

  • Your email address, when you sign up for the free preview or the paid sheet.
  • Subscription details, if you pay: your plan, its status and your payment history. Stripe handles the payment. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Email activity. Our email service may record whether an issue was delivered, opened or clicked.
  • What you send us, if you reply to one of our emails.

This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics. Like any website, the companies that host it and serve its fonts receive your IP address when your browser loads the page.

If we emailed you first

We sometimes write to agencies that may want the sheet. If you heard from us without signing up, we found your business contact details in a public government source, such as a state contract vendor list or the U.S. Small Business Administration's Small Business Search. Every such email has an unsubscribe link. Use it, or reply "no thanks", and we will not email you again.

How we use it

  • To send the issues you signed up for.
  • To run your subscription and answer your questions.
  • To keep a list of people who asked us to stop, so that we do.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

Who handles it for us

  • Buttondown sends the newsletter and holds the subscriber list.
  • Stripe processes payments.
  • Instantly sends our outreach emails.
  • GitHub hosts this website, and Google serves its fonts.

Each one handles data under its own privacy policy.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe with the link at the bottom of any email. It takes effect right away.
  • Ask us what we hold about you, or to correct or delete it. We will do it within 30 days, except for records we must keep, such as payment records and the list of people who opted out.

How long we keep it

We keep your email address for as long as you are subscribed. After you leave, we keep only what we need to honor your opt-out and to meet tax and accounting rules.

Contact

Reply to any email from us, or write to Cold Zone Studios, 22 Hancock Street, Boston, MA 02114.

Changes
